Privacy

What this site records.

This portfolio counts its visits with PostHog, to see which pages and which work get read. The count is anonymous: no cookies, nothing stored in your browser, nothing that tells who you are.

Who handles it

PostHog Inc. collects the statistics for this site. They are stored in the EU, in Frankfurt.

What is recorded

  • the pages you view, and the page you came from (the referrer)
  • clicks on links and buttons, and where on the page clicks and the pointer land, for a heatmap
  • how far down a page you scroll and how long it stays open
  • browser and device type, screen size, language and time zone
  • how fast the page loads

Your IP address

It is not stored. It is hashed together with your browser’s user agent and a salt that changes every day, only to count unique visitors per day, and then discarded.

What is not done

  • no cookies, and nothing is saved in your browser’s storage
  • no session recordings
  • no identification: visits are not tied to a name, an account or a profile
  • no advertising, and the data is never sold

How long, and on what basis

The statistics are kept for up to one year. The legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): knowing which parts of this portfolio are read, with as little data as that takes.
