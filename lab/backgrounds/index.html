01 Static wash · light

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: 4 lines of CSS Motion: none Risk: none
Three soft radials over the page ground. Nothing animates, nothing loads, nothing can drop a frame. It reads as colour rather than as an effect — which is usually what you want behind hiring-manager reading.

02 Slow drift · light

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: 3 elements, CSS only Motion: 34–47s loops Risk: low
The same wash with each blob on its own slow loop. Only transform and opacity animate, so it stays on the compositor and never touches layout. Deliberately slower than you notice: you register that it moved, not that it is moving.

03 Aurora · light

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: 1 element Motion: 60s rotation Risk: blur is heavy on low-end phones
One conic gradient turning behind a 110px blur, with a vignette so its edge never shows. The iridescence you asked about, at the lowest possible price — but a blur that large is the one thing here that can cost frames on an old device.

04 Wash + grain · light

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: one inline SVG, ~0.4 KB Motion: none Risk: none
Study 01 with film grain multiplied over it. Grain is what keeps a large gradient from looking like plastic, and it costs nothing — no request, no motion. If you ship one thing from this page, this is my pick.

05 Embers · dark

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: canvas, ~70 lines Motion: 26 particles Risk: needs a full dark mode
Twenty-six embers, not two hundred — sparse enough to read as restraint rather than as a screensaver. It is the best-looking thing on this page and the most expensive: see the note at the end.

06 Starfield · dark

Leading product design.

Currently building through an AI-native design-to-code pipeline.

Cost: canvas, ~60 lines Motion: twinkle + pointer parallax Risk: needs a full dark mode
Move the pointer: the field shifts against itself in three depths. Quieter than the embers and easier to live with behind text, but it is the more generic of the two — a starfield reads as a template more readily than ash does.
